# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Argue about ideas, not about the person
holding them.

## Standards

**Expected:** treating people with respect regardless of background or
experience level; giving and receiving criticism gracefully; assuming good
intent while still saying when something is wrong; owning mistakes.

**Not acceptable:** harassment of any kind; personal attacks; demeaning or
exclusionary language; publishing others' private information; sustained
disruption.

## A note on this project

SafeGuard is about content moderation, so discussions here will touch harassment,
hate speech, and self-harm as *technical subject matter*. That is expected and
necessary — you cannot build a moderation system without discussing what it
moderates.

The distinction is between analysing harmful content and producing it. Examples
in issues, tests, and documentation should be the mildest thing that makes the
point. Where a realistic example is genuinely needed, prefer a description of the
pattern over a reproduction of it.

Disagreement about where a policy line belongs is a legitimate and important
discussion. Using that discussion as cover for advocacy of harm is not.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer via GitHub. Reports are handled privately.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this document, and may
ban contributors for behaviour they judge inappropriate, threatening, or
harmful.
